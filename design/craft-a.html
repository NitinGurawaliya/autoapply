Direction A · Same brand, refined

Lime stays the one accent, and only means "act here" or "good". Everything that made the old screens read as a template is gone; the content from Phase M is unchanged.

  • One family (Instrument Sans) at five sizes: 13, 15, 17, 24, 32
  • Three radii: 6 tags, 12 controls, 20 cards
  • One soft lift on the card; no offset shadows, no 2 px ink borders
  • Facts as a labelled grid instead of a row of pills
  • Sentence-case labels; nothing in letter-spaced capitals
  • Buttons 48 px; lime only on Apply and the match number
  • Rose only for a required skill you lack; nice-to-have gaps are outlined
  • No stickers or rotated stamps; the deck shows one card behind
