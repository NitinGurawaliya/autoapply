Job page v2: why this score

Phase M1 mockup for /jobs/[id] (docs/job-match/TDD.md §5): the same Capco job as card 2 in design/job-card.html. Neutral by default; lime = you have it / it's good, rose = a gap. Nothing else is coloured.

  1. Header: the score ring fills to the score (ink in light, lime in dark, like today's progress bars); the tier pill sits by the posted date. Facts as grey chips; "Pay not published" is a quiet outline.
  2. Matches (lime ticks) are the score's parts at 70 % or more of their maximum; gaps (rose crosses) are parts under 50 %, any cap that lowered the score, and the required skills you don't show.
  3. Skills: lime = on your resume, rose = required and not on it, outline = only mentioned. One ink bar for coverage.
  4. How we got to 66: one ink bar per part; only a part under half its maximum shows its points in rose. The rows add up to the card's number.
