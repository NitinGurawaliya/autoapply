Direction B · New identity

Warm paper and ink with one orange accent, used only for Apply and the match number. Meaning comes from form instead of colour tints, and the job page reads like a document rather than a stack of boxes.

  • One family (Schibsted Grotesk) at five sizes: 13, 15, 17, 24, 32
  • Radii 10 for controls and logos, 16 for the card; no tags at all
  • The score shows its three parts as one split bar under the header
  • Facts as four icon lines; skills as plain text
  • Missing required skill: ink dashed underline; nice-to-have you lack: quiet grey
  • Job page sections are open, separated by rules; no panels
  • Skip is a quiet outline; Apply is the only filled button
  • Company logo on an ink tile; no stickers or stamps
