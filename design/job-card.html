Job card v2: decide in a glance

Phase M1 mockup (docs/job-match/TDD.md §5). Neutral by default; colour carries meaning only. Lime = you have it, it's good. Rose = a gap. Nothing else is coloured.

  1. Score: the exact number, big, in ink. The tier pill under it: Strong fit = lime, Good fit = grey, Stretch = dashed outline.
  2. Chips are all grey, with two exceptions: pay is outlined (it matters most, so it stands out by weight, not colour), and an experience ask over yours turns rose.
  3. Skills: lime with a check = on your profile or resume; rose = required and not on your resume ("Not on resume" when we can't tell required from optional). Left out when the posting names no tech.
  4. Why: a quiet grey box, the agent's one line.
  5. Height: 476 px here; proposed clamp(440px, calc(100dvh - 330px), 490px), with the reason at 1 line under 460 px.
